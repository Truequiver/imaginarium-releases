1 · Try it

“Try it in your browser” opens the whole pack as a view-only city. Walk the worlds, take the tours, try the Checkpoints. Nothing to install.

2 · Get the app

Download Imaginarium for Windows. It works offline, so it suits classrooms with poor internet.

3 · Add the pack

In Imaginarium: File ▸ Open file, choose the pack, then Add to my city. Pick a learner to save progress.

Every item in a pack lists who made it, its source and its license (the Credits button). Web videos are linked, not stored. About Imaginarium.
